## 1. Project Vision

RewardBench compares how GPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok change a controller when they see objective numerical rewards and penalties. Adaptation is in-context. Model weights are not trained.

Environments, scored separately:

- Gymnasium LunarLander-v3
- ApolloLander, from Seb Lee-Delisle's MIT-licensed game, with its own physics and score
- Later, and only after M1–M3: Minecraft AI Civilization

A hand-written controller is not evidence that a model adapted.

## 12. Research Methodology and Constraints

- In-context rewards only. No weight updates.
- Gymnasium scores and ApolloLander scores are never pooled.
- Development logs are not held-out evidence.
- Do not treat a scripted or hand-tuned controller as model learning.
- ApolloLander's fixed map and spawn are fine for a demonstration. They are not a sample of terrains. Generalization needs withheld initial conditions or arenas.
- Do not invent logs, rankings, or pilot outcomes.
- Do not call a paid API without an approved cap.
- Keep contract 1.0.0 stable unless the M1 merge requires a version bump. That bump belongs to Agent 1.
